If it rolls in, they fix it

Lincolnville · Staff report

For twenty years, folks in and around Lincolnville have brought just about everything with an engine to the shop on 290th Street. Commuter cars, farm pickups, semis, tractors and school buses all come through the same doors.

The shop handles diagnostics, engine and drivetrain work, brakes and suspension, electrical and A/C, tires, and routine maintenance on both gas and diesel equipment.

Customers say the approach hasn't changed since day one: explain the problem, quote it fairly, and do the job right the first time.

The shop is open weekdays and Saturday mornings. Those needing service are encouraged to call ahead or simply stop by.
