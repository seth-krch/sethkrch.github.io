      The oven

      What goes into one pizza
      Dough
      Type 00 flour, water, salt and yeast. Fermented cold for 72 hours, then stretched by hand. Nothing is rolled.
      Fire
      Split oak burns at one side of the dome. The floor holds 900°F or more, and each pizza is turned by hand through the bake.
      Toppings
      Canned San Marzano tomatoes crushed by hand, fior di latte pulled in the kitchen each morning, and basil from a local grower.
