Questions

Common questions

Do you see kids?

Yes. We recommend a first visit by age one, and we keep it short and fun.

What if I have a dental emergency?

Call as early as you can. We hold time each day for toothaches, chips and broken teeth.

I'm nervous about the dentist. Can you help?

Tell us when you book. We'll go at your pace, explain each step, and stop whenever you need.

Do I need insurance?

No. We offer cash discounts and payment plans for patients without coverage.
